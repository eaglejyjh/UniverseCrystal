# Universe Crystal Research Notes

## Overview

The Universe Crystal Research Notes record the ongoing exploration of the Universe Crystal project.

Each note captures a specific idea, discovery, or research milestone during the development of the framework.

Unlike the Canonical Baseline, Research Notes are working documents and may later be refined or incorporated into official publications.

---

## Scope

Research Notes may include:

- New concepts
- Theoretical exploration
- Physical interpretation
- Philosophical discussion
- Research planning
- Responses to feedback

---

## Numbering

Research Notes are published sequentially.

Example:

```
research-note-001.md
research-note-002.md
...
```
