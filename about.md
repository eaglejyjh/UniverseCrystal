# About

## Universe Crystal

Universe Crystal is an open long-term philosophical framework for understanding the organizational structure of the continuously evolving universe.

The project explores how existence, identity, and organization emerge through geometry and stable closure.

Its purpose is not to replace modern physics.

Instead, it provides a philosophical framework for understanding the continuously evolving universe as a whole.

---

## Research Principles

The project follows two fundamental principles:

- The Principle of Minimal Axioms
- The Principle of Introducing as Few New First Principles as Possible
