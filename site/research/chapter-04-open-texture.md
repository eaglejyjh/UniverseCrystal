# Chapter 4. Open Texture

## 4.1 The First-Principle Object

Every theory of the universe must begin by answering one fundamental question:

> **What is the most fundamental organizational object of the universe?**

Different theoretical frameworks offer different answers.

In classical mechanics, the fundamental object is typically regarded as the particle.

In quantum field theory, the field is considered more fundamental than the particle, with particles interpreted as excitations of underlying fields.

In general relativity, space-time geometry serves as the fundamental framework through which gravitation is understood.

Each theory is therefore built upon its own choice of **First-Principle Object**.

Universe Crystal adopts a different perspective.

Within this framework, the most fundamental object of the universe is neither a particle, nor a field, nor space, nor time.

Instead, Universe Crystal defines the most fundamental organizational object as **Open Texture**.

Open Texture is neither a substance, nor energy, nor a physical field.

Nor should it be understood as a trajectory in space.

It is the **Fundamental Organizational Texture** of the **Universe Crystal** and serves as the **First-Principle Object** upon which the entire framework is constructed.

---

## 4.2 Open Texture: The Geometric Existence of an Organizational Interaction

Describing Open Texture merely as an organizational texture does not answer a more fundamental question:

> **What is Open Texture?**

Universe Crystal defines it as follows:

> **Open Texture is the geometric existence of an Organizational Interaction.**

Whenever organizations interact, the interaction itself does not produce a new substance, release a new form of energy, or generate a new physical field.

Instead, the interaction possesses a geometric existence.

This geometric existence is called **Open Texture**.

Accordingly, Open Texture is not a trace left behind after an interaction has occurred.

Nor is it the trajectory recorded by an observer.

Rather, Open Texture is the geometric existence of the interaction itself.

In other words,

every **Organizational Interaction** possesses a geometric existence.

This geometric existence is referred to as **Open Texture**.

Within the Universe Crystal framework, Open Texture is therefore not the consequence of an event.

It is the organizational geometry of the event itself.

---

## 4.3 Why Open Texture Belongs to the Whole Crystal

Conventional physical descriptions often interpret interactions as local processes.

An object moves from one location to another.

A signal propagates from one point to another.

Universe Crystal adopts a fundamentally different interpretation.

Because Open Texture is the geometric existence of an **Organizational Interaction** itself, it cannot be confined to a particular local position.

Instead, it belongs inherently to the **Whole Crystal**.

From the perspective of the **Whole Crystal**, an Open Texture always exists as a complete organizational relationship.

Consequently, there is no such state as

> **"halfway propagated,"**

or

> **"partially completed."**

What is commonly interpreted as propagation is instead the progressive establishment of macroscopic relationships through **Organizational Reading**.

Open Texture itself always belongs to the whole.

It is therefore not a local object, but an integral component of the organizational structure of the **Whole Crystal**.

---

## 4.4 Why Open Texture Does Not Propagate

Propagation ordinarily implies that an object moves continuously through space.

Open Texture, however, is not a moving object.

It is neither a particle,

nor a wave,

nor a spatial trajectory.

Open Texture is the complete geometric existence of an **Organizational Interaction**.

Accordingly, it does not undergo propagation in the conventional physical sense.

What is described as propagation is instead the macroscopic interpretation established through **Organizational Reading** across different organizational levels.

From the perspective of the Universe Crystal framework, Open Texture always exists as a complete organizational relationship within the **Whole Crystal**.

There is therefore no beginning of propagation,

no end of propagation,

and no intermediate state of propagation.

---

## 4.5 Why Open Texture Is Not Space

Human intuition has long regarded space as the fundamental background of the universe.

Objects are assumed to exist in space.

Motion is assumed to occur through space.

Universe Crystal does not adopt this assumption.

Open Texture possesses neither length,

nor distance,

nor direction.

Space is therefore not the prerequisite for the existence of Open Texture.

Instead, space emerges as the macroscopic geometric manifestation of increasingly complex organizational structures.

Open Texture exists first.

Space subsequently emerges as organizational relationships become progressively richer.

Accordingly, **Space** is not a **First-Principle Object**.

---

## 4.6 Why Open Texture Is Not Time

Time is likewise not an attribute of Open Texture.

As the geometric existence of an **Organizational Interaction**, Open Texture possesses neither past nor future.

Nor does it experience the passage of time.

Consider an Open Texture associated with a distant galaxy.

From the perspective of the **Whole Crystal**, it always belongs to the same unified organizational whole.

The statement that it has "traveled for millions of years" represents only a macroscopic relationship established through **Organizational Reading**.

Time is therefore not something experienced by Open Texture.

Rather, it is a higher-level description generated through **Organizational Reading**.

Open Texture belongs to the **Whole Crystal** itself, not to any particular moment in time.

---

## 4.7 Open Texture as the Fundamental Organizational Texture

Open Texture not only defines the geometric existence of **Organizational Interactions**.

It also constitutes the **Fundamental Organizational Texture** of the **Universe Crystal**.

The term *texture* should not be interpreted as physical matter.

Instead, it denotes the most fundamental organizational substrate upon which all higher organizational structures are established.

Through **Stable Closure**, Open Texture gives rise to **Stable Closed Texture**.

Stable Closed Textures subsequently organize into increasingly higher organizational levels.

Accordingly, Open Texture is not the product of organization.

It is the common organizational foundation upon which all subsequent organization depends.

In this sense, it provides the fundamental organizational continuity of the **Universe Crystal**.

---

## 4.8 The Theoretical Significance of Open Texture

The introduction of Open Texture enables Universe Crystal to describe the universe through a unified organizational language.

**Space** is not a **First-Principle Object**.

**Time** is not a **First-Principle Object**.

Particles are not **First-Principle Objects**.

Energy is not a **First-Principle Object**.

The universe is understood first and foremost as organization.

Open Texture represents the most fundamental geometric existence of **Organizational Interactions**.

From this foundation,

**Stable Closed Texture** gives rise to the first stable forms of existence.

**Organization Hierarchy** explains the emergence of increasingly complex structures.

**Space**,

**Time**,

and the classical world

are ultimately understood as macroscopic manifestations produced through **Organizational Reading**.

Open Texture therefore serves not only as the **First-Principle Object** of Universe Crystal, but also as the logical point of departure for the entire theoretical framework.

---

## 4.9 Conclusion

Open Texture is neither a substance,

nor energy,

nor a physical field,

nor a trajectory in space.

It is the geometric existence of an **Organizational Interaction**.

Every **Organizational Interaction** gives rise to an **Open Texture**.

Open Texture inherently belongs to the **Whole Crystal**.

Consequently,

there is no halfway propagation,

no partially completed interaction,

and no local existence independent of the whole.

**Space** is not a **First-Principle Object**.

**Time** is not a **First-Principle Object**.

**Stable Closed Texture**,

**Organization Hierarchy**,

**Space**,

**Time**,

and the classical world

are all ultimately founded upon the continuous organization of **Open Texture**.

Open Texture therefore constitutes both the **Fundamental Organizational Texture** of the **Universe Crystal** and the logical foundation upon which the entire framework is built.
