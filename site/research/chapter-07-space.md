# Chapter 7. Space

## 7.1 Is Space a First-Principle Object?

From everyday human experience, space appears to exist naturally.

Objects exist in space.

Motion takes place through space.

It is therefore intuitive to regard space as the fundamental background of the universe.

Modern physics has profoundly transformed this intuitive picture. General relativity, for example, unifies space and time into space-time and demonstrates that the geometry of space-time is dynamic rather than fixed.

Nevertheless, space-time remains a fundamental component of contemporary physical theories.

Universe Crystal proposes a different interpretation.

Within this framework,

> **Space is not a First-Principle Object.**

It is not the primordial background upon which the universe is constructed.

Nor is it the prerequisite for the existence of organizations.

Instead, space is understood as a macroscopic property that emerges through the progressive development of organizational structures.

Accordingly, Universe Crystal does not begin with space.

It begins with organization, from which space subsequently emerges.

---

## 7.2 Open Texture Has No Spatial Properties

The **First-Principle Object** of Universe Crystal is **Open Texture**.

Open Texture is not a line in space.

Nor is it a trajectory propagating through space.

Open Texture possesses neither:

- Length;
- Distance;
- Direction; nor
- Physical volume.

Consequently, Open Texture has no spatial relationships in the conventional sense.

Concepts such as position, distance, and orientation are not intrinsic properties of Open Texture itself.

Instead, Open Texture belongs fundamentally to the **Whole Crystal**.

Spatial relationships emerge only as higher organizational levels are formed.

Space is therefore not a prerequisite for the existence of Open Texture.

---

## 7.3 First-Level Stable Closure Has No Spatial Degrees of Freedom

When **Open Texture** undergoes **Stable Closure**, it gives rise to **First-Level Stable Closed Texture**.

At this organizational level, stable **Identity** has already emerged.

Physical volume, however, has not.

Accordingly, **First-Level Stable Closure** possesses no **Spatial Degrees of Freedom**.

Within the current framework, electrons and quarks are interpreted as **First-Level Stable Closed Textures**.

Their point-like manifestation should not be understood as evidence that they are infinitely small objects.

Rather, it reflects the fact that space has not yet emerged at this organizational level.

**First-Level Stable Closure** therefore establishes existence,

but not space.

---

## 7.4 Spatial Degrees of Freedom First Emerge in Second-Level Organization

Universe Crystal proposes that **Spatial Degrees of Freedom** first emerge at the level of **Second-Level Organization**.

When multiple **First-Level Stable Closed Textures** organize into a stable organizational whole, the resulting organization acquires, for the first time:

- A stable scale;
- Stable organizational boundaries; and
- Physical volume.

These newly emerged organizational properties give rise to the first spatial characteristics.

Space should therefore not be interpreted as a pre-existing container within which organizations exist.

Instead, it is the macroscopic geometric manifestation of stable organizational relationships.

Accordingly,

> **Space emerges from organization, rather than organization occurring within space.**

This represents one of the central principles of the Universe Crystal interpretation of space.

---

## 7.5 Why Does the Macroscopic World Appear Three-Dimensional?

Within the current Universe Crystal framework, the three-dimensional character of macroscopic space is not treated as a fundamental axiom.

Instead, it is understood as the natural consequence of continued organizational development.

**Second-Level Organization** gives rise to the first stable **Spatial Degrees of Freedom**.

As increasingly higher organizational levels emerge, these stable organizational structures collectively produce the macroscopic spatial world.

The familiar three-dimensional character of space is therefore interpreted as the unified macroscopic manifestation of long-term organizational evolution.

It is not assumed as the initial background of the universe.

Rather, it emerges through organization itself.

---

## 7.6 An Organizational Interpretation of Space

If space emerges from organization, then it no longer possesses an independent ontological status.

Instead, it becomes a macroscopic geometric description of organizational relationships.

Accordingly,

space changes as organizations change.

Different organizational levels may exhibit different spatial properties.

Space is therefore not an absolute entity.

It is a unified geometric manifestation that emerges once organization reaches sufficient complexity.

From this perspective, space becomes an integral component of the organizational framework established by Universe Crystal.

It no longer exists independently of particles, interactions, or time.

Rather, all of these are interpreted as different manifestations of the continuing emergence of organization.

---

## 7.7 Conclusion

Universe Crystal does not regard **Space** as a **First-Principle Object**.

**Open Texture** possesses no spatial properties.

**First-Level Stable Closure** possesses no **Spatial Degrees of Freedom**.

**Spatial Degrees of Freedom** first emerge at the level of **Second-Level Organization**.

Stable organizations give rise to stable scale, organizational boundaries, and physical volume.

As the **Organization Hierarchy** continues to develop, the macroscopic world ultimately appears as a unified three-dimensional space.

Accordingly, space is not the primordial background of the universe.

It emerges from organization and is understood as the macroscopic geometric manifestation of the continuing organizational development of the **Whole Crystal**.
